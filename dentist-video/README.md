# Visiting the Dentist – animated story video (Grade 1)

A 3D cartoon video (about 3½ minutes) that tells the textbook reading *Visiting the Dentist*
(Chapter 2B: Helping Hands, pp. 126–130).

* **Mahino** speaks with a young girl's voice; the **Kids Mag interviewer** and the **narrator** are women.
* Every line of the reading appears as a large subtitle, so pupils can read along.
* Pop-up **new words**: receptionist, hygienist, x-ray, sunglasses, tools, fluoride, enamel, plaque, dentist.
* Scenes: waiting room → reception → Hala the hygienist → x-ray → bright light & mirror → cleaning →
  "how I help" → fluoride flavours (bubble gum!) → enamel & plaque with a tooth character →
  Dr. Alfi and the six baby teeth → goodbye.
* Ends with the book's three questions (What is an x-ray? How does Hala help Mahino feel comfortable?
  What is fluoride?), each with a thinking pause and then the answer.

Files: `visiting-the-dentist.mp4` (3D cartoon version), `visiting-the-dentist-anime.mp4` (anime version: cel shading, ink outlines, big shiny eyes, speed lines; render with `QUERY=?anime`), `script.json` (the lines), `make_audio.py` (voices + music),
`web/` (Three.js scenes), `render.js` (frame renderer), `build.sh` (rebuilds everything).
